# GARAGE — COMPLETE MODULE OVERVIEW

## Run Your Garage. Manage Your Team. Grow Your Business.

**Garage brings your customers, vehicles, sales, service operations, employees, finance and daily work into one simple platform.**

Instead of managing your garage through notebooks, spreadsheets, separate apps and WhatsApp conversations, Garage gives your team one connected workspace.

### From the first customer enquiry to the final payment — everything stays connected.

🟢 **Get Leads** → 🔵 **Convert Customers** → 🟣 **Manage Service** → 🟠 **Manage Team** → 🟡 **Track Money** → 🚀 **Grow**

# 🏠 Dashboard

### Your garage at a glance

The Garage Dashboard gives the owner and management team a quick view of what is happening today.

**See:**
- 🟢 Today's jobs
- 🔵 New enquiries
- 🟣 Active customers
- 🟠 Pending payments
- 🔴 Overdue work
- 🟡 Team activity
- 📊 Sales and business performance

### How it works
Garage collects information from your CRM, sales, projects, finance and HR modules and brings the important numbers into one screen.

### Growth impact
You spend less time checking different systems and more time making decisions.
