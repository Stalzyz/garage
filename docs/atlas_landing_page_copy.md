# SECTION 3 — THE LOCAL INTEGRATIONS ENGINE

## Visual Layout Specs
- Four-card layout with hover animations detailing the core local integrations built natively into Atlas.

## Copy Block

---

### 1. One-Click UPI Payments
- **Headline:** Frictionless Mobile Checkout
- **Copy:** 80% of Indian online shoppers prefer UPI. Atlas features native UPI deep-linking, allowing customers to pay directly from Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm with one click. No page redirects. No dropped checkouts.

---

### 2. Built-in COD Fraud Prevention
- **Headline:** Protect Your Margin From Fake Orders
- **Copy:** Cash on Delivery is essential, but return-to-origin (RTO) costs eat your profit. Atlas includes built-in COD OTP verification via SMS/WhatsApp, filtering out fake addresses before they reach your warehouse.

---

### 3. Local Shipping Integrations
- **Headline:** Shiprocket, Delhivery, and Blue Dart Sync
- **Copy:** Ship automatically. Atlas syncs directly with major Indian logistics aggregators. Generate shipping labels, schedule pickups, and update inventory instantly from a single dashboard.

---

### 4. Automatic GST Invoices
- **Headline:** Compliant Invoicing Built-in
- **Copy:** Stop compiling invoices manually at tax time. Atlas generates GST-compliant invoices for every purchase, automatically calculating CGST, SGST, and IGST based on the customer’s shipping address.
