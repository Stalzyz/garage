# SECTION 1 — What is Atlas?

Atlas is Grekam's ecommerce platform. It gives businesses a complete, branded online store that they fully own and control.

It handles:
- Product catalogue
- Storefront (what customers see)
- Admin dashboard (what the business manages)
- Orders
- Payments
- Inventory
- Customers
- Shipping
- Discounts and promotions
- Reporting

---

# SECTION 2 — Why Atlas Over Shopify

Most businesses you speak to will have heard of Shopify. You need to understand the comparison.

| Area | Shopify | Atlas |
|---|---|---|
| **Pricing** | USD-based, 2%+ transaction fees | INR pricing, no transaction fees |
| **Payment gateways** | Limited Indian UPI support | Full UPI, Razorpay, Stripe, COD built-in |
| **GST invoicing** | Requires third-party apps | Built in natively |
| **Language / locale** | English first | Indian market first |
| **Support** | Global ticketing system | Direct Grekam team access |
| **Customisation** | Theme-limited unless you pay Shopify developers | Full customisation by Grekam team |
| **Data ownership** | Shopify stores your data | Client owns data on their infrastructure |
| **Onboarding** | Self-serve, no hand-holding | Hands-on onboarding by Grekam |

**Key positioning statement:**

> "Shopify is built for global markets. Atlas is built specifically for Indian businesses — with the payment methods, tax structure, and support that Indian ecommerce actually needs."

# SECTION 3 — Who Needs Atlas

### Strong fit:
- Businesses selling physical products who want to move beyond Instagram / WhatsApp ordering
- Retail stores wanting to go online
- D2C brands (clothing, food, beauty, health, home goods)
- Wholesale businesses wanting a B2B ordering portal
- Businesses currently on outdated platforms wanting to migrate

### Weak fit (flag these):
- Businesses wanting to sell on Amazon or Flipkart (marketplaces, not their own store)
- Businesses with fewer than 10 products and no growth ambition
- Businesses that need heavy custom ERP integration (discuss feasibility first)

# SECTION 4 — The Customer Journey in Atlas

Help the customer visualise how their buyer shops. Walk them through this story:

```
Customer browses the storefront on mobile
          ↓
Finds a product — sees images, description, reviews
          ↓
Adds to cart
          ↓
Checkout — enters address, selects payment (UPI / Card / COD)
          ↓
Payment confirmed — auto-confirmation message sent
          ↓
Business admin sees the order in the dashboard
          ↓
Order processed — shipping details updated
          ↓
Customer receives tracking update
          ↓
Order delivered — review request sent
```

**During sales:** Walk this journey live in the demo. Do not just show the admin panel.

> "Before I show you how you manage the store, let me show you what your customer experiences. This is what they see on their phone."

# SECTION 5 — The 15-Minute Demo Script

### Phase 1 — Storefront First (5 minutes)

Open the Atlas demo storefront on a mobile device or mobile browser view.

> "This is what your customer sees. Notice how it loads instantly, it's mobile-first, and the checkout is clean. Most of your customers are buying on their phone — this is built for that."

Walk through:
- Homepage with featured products
- Product page (images, description, variants, add to cart)
- Cart
- Checkout flow (show UPI and COD options)

---

### Phase 2 — Admin Dashboard (7 minutes)

Open the admin panel.

> "Now this is your side — what you see when an order comes in."

Walk through:
- Orders — show a new order appearing
- Processing an order (confirm → ship → delivered)
- Inventory — show stock level updating
- Adding a product (quick walkthrough — image upload, variants, price)
- Customer records

---

### Phase 3 — Reports (2 minutes)

> "At any point you can see your sales, your best-selling products, and your customers — without needing to export to Excel."

Show:
- Revenue summary
- Top products
- Customer growth

---

### Phase 4 — Closing (1 minute)

> "You told me earlier that [their specific pain — e.g., 'you're processing orders manually on WhatsApp']. With Atlas, that entire process is automated. Your team focuses on packing and shipping. The system handles everything else."
>
> "Does this solve what you're looking for?"

# SECTION 6 — Requirements Mapping

During qualification, map their requirements to Atlas capabilities.

| Customer Requirement | Atlas Can Do | Needs Custom Development |
|---|---|---|
| Product catalogue with variants | ✅ | — |
| UPI / Card / COD payments | ✅ | — |
| Automatic order confirmation message | ✅ | — |
| Inventory management | ✅ | — |
| Discount codes | ✅ | — |
| Customer accounts and order history | ✅ | — |
| GST invoicing | ✅ | — |
| WhatsApp order updates integration | ✅ (with Grafty) | — |
| Multiple warehouses / locations | 🔶 Limited | May need custom |
| B2B pricing (different prices per customer) | 🔶 | May need custom |
| Subscription / recurring orders | ❌ | Custom development required |
| ERP integration (Tally, SAP) | ❌ | Custom development required |

**When you see a custom requirement:**
> "That's a specific requirement — let me check with our technical team before confirming. I want to make sure I give you the right answer rather than commit to something I'm not certain of."

Never say "yes we can do that" to anything in the orange or red column without technical confirmation.

# SECTION 8 — Grafty + Atlas Cross-Sell

The most natural upsell in the Grekam ecosystem.

When a customer buys Atlas:
> "One thing our Atlas clients find extremely useful is connecting it with Grafty — our WhatsApp automation. When a customer places an order on Atlas, Grafty automatically sends them a WhatsApp confirmation, shipping update, and delivery notification. Your customer feels looked after. Your team doesn't have to type a single message."

This is not a pressure sell. It is a genuine improvement to the customer experience.

# SECTION 9 — Atlas Certification Criteria

To be certified to sell Atlas:

**Level 1 — User:**
- Navigate the admin panel independently
- Add a product, process an order end to end
- Read the reports dashboard

**Level 2 — Demonstrator:**
- Conduct the full 15-minute demo independently
- Show the customer journey from storefront to admin

**Level 3 — Salesperson:**
- Successfully qualify an Atlas lead using the qualification form
- Map customer requirements to Atlas capabilities accurately
- Identify when a requirement is custom (and not promise it)

**Level 4 — Consultant:**
- Recommend the right package based on business size and requirements
- Identify natural cross-sell opportunities (Grafty, Grekam OS)
- Handle all common objections without referencing this document
