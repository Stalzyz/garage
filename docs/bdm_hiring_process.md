# BDM Hiring & Interview Playbook
### Version 1.0 | Internal HR & Recruitment Document | August 2026

---

> This playbook defines how we source, screen, interview, and select the
> **Business Development & Operations Manager**.
> We do not hire based on "good vibes" or smooth talking.
> We hire based on values alignment, coachability, and operational discipline.

---

# SECTION 1 — JOB DESCRIPTION & PROFILE

## Role Mission
To scale Grekam's revenue and operations by executing high-volume sales activities during the first 90 days, transitioning into full team management, process documentation, and operational ownership by Month 3.

---

## 90-Day Structured Performance Path

```
MONTH 1 — Frontline Execution
Sales/Telesales → Qualification → Demos → Deal Closing → Lead Logging (100% CRM)
        ↓
MONTH 2 — Transition & Audit
Process Documentation → CRM Health Checks → Project Manager Shadowing
        ↓
MONTH 3 — System Management
Training New Hires → Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) → KPI Dashboards
```

---

## Profile Requirements

| Attribute | What We Look For | Red Flags |
|---|---|---|
| **Experience** | 2-4 years in SaaS sales, digital agency sales, or account management | "Strategy only" backgrounds; no history of making cold calls |
| **Tool Skills** | Highly familiar with CRM databases, task managers, and documentation tools | Reluctance to use or log data in systems; preference for pen & paper |
| **Mindset** | Solution-oriented. When presenting a problem, always brings 3 recommendations | "What should I do next?" mindset; passive listener |
| **Communication** | Direct, clear, structured speaker who values written clarity | Vague, overly verbose, or uses jargon to avoid concrete commitments |

# SECTION 2 — PRE-SCREENING FILTERS

Send these questions to candidates before booking a call. Ask them to write brief, 2-sentence answers.

1. **The System Question:**
   > *"A client is ready to buy but hasn't paid yet. They want you to initiate setup now to save time. What do you do, and why?"*
   * **Correct answer:** Refuse setup, follow the system. If it's not paid, it doesn't proceed.
   * **Red flag:** "I would start setup to make the customer happy."

2. **The Problem Question:**
   > *"You notice a salesperson on your team has 5 overdue tasks in the CRM. How do you handle this?"*
   * **Correct answer:** Sit with them to check the CRM, find the root cause, update/reschedule tasks with notes, and enforce the daily checklist rule.
   * **Red flag:** "I would ignore it if they are hitting their numbers."

3. **The Communication Question:**
   > *"A client complains that a project is delayed by 2 days. Draft the exact message you would send them."*
   * **Correct answer:** Proactive, owns the timeline, states the reason, provides the new date, and asks if it works.
   * **Red flag:** Defensive, blames developers, or says "it will be done soon."

# SECTION 3 — ROUND 1 INTERVIEW: VALUES & CULTURE

Focus on values alignment. Use these questions to test for the core company culture:

---

## 1. Test for: Ownership vs. Learned Helplessness

> **Question:** *"Tell me about a time in your previous role when you encountered a problem that had no written solution or process. What did you do?"*

* **Look for:** They did the research, proposed options, made a decision, resolved it, and **documented the solution** for the next person.
* **Red flag:** "I asked my manager what to do and did exactly what they said."

---

## 2. Test for: Transparency vs. Hidden Problems

> **Question:** *"Imagine you realise you will miss a critical proposal deadline by 24 hours. Your manager hasn't noticed yet. How and when do you communicate this?"*

* **Look for:** Proactive communication *before* the deadline, owning the gap, and presenting a recovery plan.
* **Red flag:** "I would stay up all night to finish it and only tell them if I couldn't complete it by morning." (This masks the risk).

---

## 3. Test for: System-Driven vs. Habit-Driven

> **Question:** *"How do you keep track of your daily tasks and client interactions? What happens if you get busy and miss updates?"*

* **Look for:** Reliance on the CRM/system. "If it's not in the system, it doesn't exist." Clear daily routine.
* **Red flag:** "I keep notes in a notebook or my phone and update the CRM at the end of the week."

# SECTION 4 — ROUND 2 ROLE-PLAY SCENARIOS

The evaluator plays the roles. The candidate is evaluated live on the call.

---

## Scenario A — The Sales Call

### The Setup
The evaluator plays **Siddharth Roy**, owner of *Roy Logistics* (a traditional logistics business with 15 delivery drivers). Siddharth is a busy, direct manager who handles customer updates via manual WhatsApp messages.

### The Task
The candidate must conduct a 5-minute lead qualification and discovery call to see if he is a fit for **Grafty**.

### Evaluator Behaviors to Trigger:
- Say: *"I don't have time for a demo. Just send me your brochure and pricing on WhatsApp."*
- Say: *"Why should I pay you ₹4,500/month when I already have a free WhatsApp Business app on my phone?"*

---

### Expected Candidate Actions:
- [ ] Decline to just send a brochure without context; explain that Grafty is tailored to his specific team setup.
- [ ] Ask: *"How many messages is your team sending per day, and how long does it take them?"* (Discovery first).
- [ ] Explain the limitation of the WhatsApp App (1 phone vs. 15 drivers) and contrast it with Grafty's multi-user inbox.
- [ ] Secure a concrete next action (demo time/day) before ending the call.

---

## Scenario B — The Conflict Escalation Call

### The Setup
The evaluator plays **Nisha Sen**, owner of *Sen Apparel*. She paid a 50% deposit for Atlas 3 weeks ago. She is angry because her storefront launch was promised for today, but the developer informed her it will take 3 more days due to a billing integration delay.

### The Task
The candidate must handle her incoming angry phone call.

### Evaluator Behaviors to Trigger:
- Shout / use aggressive tone: *"This is unacceptable! Your salesperson told me it would be live today. I have ads scheduled to run tomorrow. I want a refund now!"*
- Say: *"I don't care about your developers' excuses. Who is taking responsibility for this?"*

---

### Expected Candidate Actions:
- [ ] Listen completely without interrupting or making excuses.
- [ ] Acknowledge the frustration immediately: *"I understand you have ads scheduled and this delay directly impacts your launch. You are completely right to be frustrated."*
- [ ] Take personal responsibility on behalf of Grekam: *"I am owning this issue now."*
- [ ] Decline to issue an immediate refund on the spot, but promise investigation: *"I will investigate the exact state of the gateway integration now and call you back by 5pm today with the recovery plan."*
- [ ] Log the escalation in the system immediately after the call.

# SECTION 5 — BD/OPERATIONS EVALUATION SCORECARD

Rate the candidate on a scale of **1 (Weak) to 5 (Outstanding)** across these five criteria during the role-play and interview.

---

```
CANDIDATE NAME: ___________________________
EVALUATOR NAME: ___________________________
DATE:           ___________________________

1. PROCESS DISCIPLINE (1 - 5)
   Does the candidate respect system workflows, or do they look for shortcuts?
   [ ] 1: Prefers manual tracking, bypasses guidelines to make sales.
   [ ] 3: Follows guidelines but needs prompts to log notes.
   [ ] 5: System-first advocate. Understands data is the foundation.

2. DISCOVERY & QUALIFICATION (1 - 5)
   Did they ask questions first, or did they jump into a generic pitch?
   [ ] 1: Pitched features immediately. Did not qualify Siddharth's business.
   [ ] 3: Asked basic questions but missed the team-size bottleneck.
   [ ] 5: Structured discovery. Mapped Grafty value directly to Roy's team pain.

3. CONFLICT CONFLICT RESOLUTION (1 - 5)
   How did they handle Nisha's anger? Did they freeze, get defensive, or take ownership?
   [ ] 1: Argued, blamed the developer, or promised an immediate refund.
   [ ] 3: Apologised repeatedly but failed to define the next update step.
   [ ] 5: Took ownership, validated her frustration, set a clear follow-up time.

4. SCOPE PROTECTION (1 - 5)
   Did they protect company limits, or did they make unconfirmed promises?
   [ ] 1: Promised customization or instant fixes to appease the caller.
   [ ] 3: Deferential, but hesitant when handling pricing/timeline objections.
   [ ] 5: Confident in value. Held boundaries on out-of-scope requests politely.

5. COACHABILITY (1 - 5)
   How did they respond when given feedback after the role-play?
   [ ] 1: Defensive. Explained why their way was correct.
   [ ] 3: Acknowledged feedback but did not demonstrate how they would apply it.
   [ ] 5: Immediate reflection. Re-pitched the segment using the feedback.

TOTAL SCORE: ___ / 25
```

## Hiring Decision Rubric

* **22–25:** **Hire immediately.** High discipline, strong values, excellent operational capacity.
* **17–21:** **Conditional hire.** Good sales skills, but requires close monitoring on CRM logging and scope management in Month 1.
* **Below 17:** **Reject.** Do not proceed. Risk of process decay and customer escalations is too high.
