We build:
- **Grafty** — WhatsApp automation for businesses
- **Atlas** — Ecommerce platform for Indian brands
- **Grekam OS** — Business operating platform for internal management
- **Custom web applications** — Built for specific business problems

We work with businesses across India who are trying to grow, digitise, and systematise how they operate.

We are not in the business of simply completing tasks.

We are in the business of building something that lasts.

# WHAT KIND OF COMPANY WE ARE BUILDING

We are building a company where:

- The system runs the business, not the individuals
- Documentation replaces verbal repetition
- Every person owns their work end-to-end
- Problems surface early, not after damage is done
- Revenue and profitability both matter — not just activity
- Customers are relationships, not transactions
- Growth is real — not just in title but in actual capability

If you are looking for a place where you follow instructions and clock out, this is not it.

If you are looking for a place where you can grow into a bigger role, earn more as the company grows, and eventually own meaningful pieces of a business operation — this is where that happens.

# HOW WE MAKE DECISIONS

We make decisions at the lowest level possible.

If you can make the decision, make it.
If you are unsure, check the relevant document, the system, or a colleague.
If it is beyond your authority, escalate — but come with a recommendation.

We do not make decisions by committee.
We do not wait for founder approval on operational matters.
We do not discuss decisions that one person should simply be making.

The higher the risk, the more we escalate.
The more routine the decision, the more we expect you to own it.

**The test for any decision:**

> "If this went wrong, would the company survive? Would a customer be seriously affected? Would there be a financial or legal consequence?"

If no — make the call.
If yes — escalate with your recommendation.

# WHAT WE DO NOT TOLERATE

These are not rules we enforce through warnings.
They are values that determine fit.

---

**We do not tolerate learned helplessness.**

Showing up with "what should I do?" for every small problem is not being careful. It is avoiding responsibility. We invest in people who try, fail, learn, and improve — not people who wait for instructions.

---

**We do not tolerate hidden problems.**

A problem that surfaces early is manageable.
A problem that surfaces after the deadline is a crisis.
If something is going wrong, say it early. There is no punishment for honest early escalation. There is no tolerance for hiding bad news.

---

**We do not tolerate overpromising.**

Telling a customer "yes, we can do that" when you do not know whether we can — is not closing a deal. It is creating a future problem for the team and a bad experience for the customer. We would rather lose a sale than fail to deliver what we promised.

---

**We do not tolerate working around the system.**

If it is not in Grekam OS, it does not exist for this company. Excel sheets, personal notebooks, and memory are not company infrastructure. Data belongs in the system.

---

**We do not tolerate disrespect.**

Toward customers, toward colleagues, toward vendors.
We compete on quality and integrity — not on who shouts loudest.

# HOW WE MEASURE PEOPLE

We do not measure people primarily by how busy they are.

We measure by:

### Output
What did you produce? What changed because of your work?

### Quality
Was the work done well? Would it hold up to scrutiny?

### Ownership
Did you manage the entire thing — or did you hand it off the moment it got difficult?

### Growth
Are you more capable than you were 3 months ago?

### Contribution to the system
Did you document what you learned? Can someone else use what you built?

# WHAT GROWTH LOOKS LIKE HERE

Growth at Grekam is not automatic. It is earned.

You grow when:
- You demonstrate that you can handle more
- You build things that work without you
- You train others to do what you do
- You take on problems that weren't assigned to you
- You make management's job easier, not harder

Titles change when responsibility changes.
Compensation changes when your contribution changes.

We do not give promotions based on time served.
We give them based on what you have built and proven.

# HOW WE COMMUNICATE

**We communicate clearly and directly.**
Not bluntly. Not harshly. But directly.

If there is a problem, we name it.
If there is a disagreement, we discuss it.
We do not hint, sulk, or communicate through passive behaviour.

**We write things down.**
If it is important, it is documented.
Conversations that happen and disappear are not a reliable foundation for a growing business.

**We update without being asked.**
Your manager should not need to ask "what is the status?" regularly.
If you are working on something, the system reflects it.
If something changed, you communicate it proactively.

# THE 10 RULES WE OPERATE BY

These are not aspirational. These are operational.

1. **If it is not in Grekam OS, it does not exist.**

2. **Never make a promise the company cannot deliver.**

3. **Never hide a problem. Surface it early.**

4. **Do not bring problems without solutions.**

5. **Understand the customer's problem before selling a product.**

6. **Revenue without profit is not success.**

7. **Every active lead must have a next action.**

8. **Anything explained repeatedly must become documentation.**

9. **Build systems, not dependencies on individuals.**

10. **Your job is not just to do the work. It is to make the work better.**

# WHAT YOUR FIRST 90 DAYS LOOK LIKE

We do not throw people into the deep end.

Your first 90 days are structured intentionally:

**Month 1 — Learn.**
Understand the company, the products, the customers, and the tools. No performance pressure — learning pressure only.

**Month 2 — Do.**
You begin doing real work with real accountability. You are expected to perform independently.

**Month 3 — Build.**
You begin building systems, documentation, and capability — not just completing tasks.

After 90 days, we evaluate together: Where are you? What's next?
