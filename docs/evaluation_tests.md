# Grekam OS — Evaluation & Certification System
### Internal HR & Management Document | August 2026

---

> This document defines how we verify that every team member
> has truly learned Grekam OS — not just read about it.
>
> Reading is not training.
> Performance is training.

---

# EVALUATION OVERVIEW

| Evaluation | When | Conducted By | Pass Standard |
|---|---|---|---|

# EVALUATION 1 — THE DAY 7 SETUP TEST

**Purpose:** Verify that the employee can correctly create and populate records in Grekam OS.

**When:** End of Day 7 of employment.

**Duration:** 20 to 30 minutes.

**Format:** The evaluator reads out a fictional scenario. The employee enters all information into Grekam OS live. The evaluator then reviews the records without the employee explaining anything further.

---

## Scenario A — Salesperson / BDM

Read this to the employee:

> "A woman named Ananya Krishnan called you today at 2pm. She owns a business called Fresh Bloom Organics in Bangalore. They sell organic skincare products online and through 3 retail stores. She found us through Instagram. She wants to set up WhatsApp automation to send order confirmations and shipping updates to her customers. She currently does this manually and has 4 staff managing WhatsApp. She told you her budget is around ₹4,000 to ₹5,000 per month. She is the founder and sole decision-maker. She said she wants to get started before September 15th. She is open to a product demo. She asked you to call her Thursday at 3pm to confirm a demo time."
>
> "Enter this correctly into Grekam OS. You have 15 minutes."

---

### Scoring Rubric — Scenario A

| Field | Correct Entry | Score |
|---|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Ananya Krishnan (not "Ananya" or "client") | 1 |
| **Company + Context** | Fresh Bloom Organics, Bangalore, organic skincare, online + 3 retail stores | 1 |
| **Source** | Instagram | 1 |
| **Requirement** | WhatsApp automation for order confirmations and shipping updates. 4 staff managing WhatsApp manually. | 1 |
| **Next Action with Date** | Call Thursday [specific date] at 3pm to confirm demo time | 1 |
| **Notes with Full Context** | Budget ₹4-5k/month. Decision-maker confirmed (founder). Timeline: before 15 Sept. Interested in demo. | 1 |

**PASS: 5/6 or higher.**

**FAIL: 4/6 or lower.** Repeat training on Lead Entry and re-test Monday.

---

## Scenario B — Operations / Support

Read this to the employee:

> "A customer named Rohan Mehta from Mehta Auto Parts has called with a complaint. They purchased Grafty two weeks ago. They are saying that their WhatsApp template for order updates was rejected and they don't know why. The issue was first reported yesterday but nobody has followed up yet. He is frustrated. His account manager is Priya. His plan is the Grafty Business Plan at ₹4,500/month. He has paid for two months upfront."
>
> "Log this correctly in Grekam OS. You have 15 minutes."

---

### Scoring Rubric — Scenario B

| Field | Correct Entry | Score |
|---|---|---|
| **Issue logged as a Task** | Task created, not just a note | 1 |
| **Linked to Customer Record** | Task is linked to Mehta Auto Parts customer record | 1 |
| **Assigned To** | Priya (account manager) or appropriate support owner | 1 |
| **Issue Description** | Template rejection issue. First reported yesterday. Not yet followed up. Customer is frustrated. | 1 |
| **Priority** | High (customer is frustrated, already delayed one day) | 1 |
| **Due Date** | Today or specific date within 24 hours | 1 |

**PASS: 5/6 or higher.**

# EVALUATION 2 — THE WEEK 3 LIVE AUDIT

**Purpose:** Verify that the employee's actual working entries match their verbal understanding — that the system reflects reality, not just what they tell you.

**When:** Randomly selected day in Week 3. Do not announce in advance.

**Duration:** 15 to 20 minutes.

**Format:** Open the employee's records in Grekam OS while they are present. Ask them to walk you through their active leads, tasks, or projects. Compare what they say verbally with what is in the system.

---

## Questions to Ask During the Audit

For each active lead:

> "Tell me the current status of this lead."
> "When did you last speak to them?"
> "What was the outcome of that conversation?"
> "What happens next and when?"

After they answer verbally — check the system.

---

## Audit Pass / Fail Criteria

| What you observe | Result |
|---|---|
| Verbal answer matches CRM data exactly | ✅ Pass |
| Verbal answer contains information NOT in CRM | ❌ Fail — data is being kept out of system |
| CRM has a field blank that should not be blank | ❌ Fail — incomplete entries |
| Follow-up dates in the past with no action taken | ❌ Fail — discipline issue |
| Employee hesitates and cannot answer from memory (but CRM has the answer) | ✅ Pass — this is the correct outcome. They are depending on the system. |

---

## If the Employee Fails the Audit

Do not reprimand in the moment.

After the session:

> "I noticed that [specific information] was in your memory but not in Grekam OS. What was the reason?"

Listen to the answer. Then:

> "The standard is: if you would rely on your memory to answer a question about a customer, that information needs to be in the system. What will you do differently going forward?"

Schedule a follow-up audit for the following week.

# EVALUATION 3 — THE MONTH 1 CONTINUITY TEST

**Purpose:** Test whether the company can operate without this employee for 48 hours using only Grekam OS data.

**When:** Final day of Month 1 (or within 3 days of it).

**Duration:** 30 minutes.

**Format:** Tell the employee you will attempt to manage their work from Grekam OS for the next business day without contacting them. Ask them to ensure everything is ready for this.

Give them 30 minutes to update their records.

Then, without asking the employee anything, attempt to answer the following:

---

## Continuity Test Questions

For a Salesperson:
```
1. What leads are scheduled for follow-up tomorrow?
2. For each active deal — what is the current status and what is the next step?
3. Is there anything that must happen today before end of day?
4. Which deals are closest to closing and what is needed to close them?
5. Are there any customer issues that need attention?
```

For a Developer:
```
1. What tasks are due this week?
2. For each in-progress task — where is it and what remains?
3. Are there any blockers on any project?
4. What would I need to communicate to clients today based on project status?
5. Is there anything urgent that cannot wait?
```

---

## Continuity Test Scoring

| Question | Answerable from CRM alone | Notes needed from employee |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | ✅ / ❌ | |
| Q2 | ✅ / ❌ | |
| Q3 | ✅ / ❌ | |
| Q4 | ✅ / ❌ | |
| Q5 | ✅ / ❌ | |

**PASS: 4/5 answerable from CRM alone.**

**FAIL: 3/5 or fewer answerable.** Means the employee is holding information in their head, not the system.

---

## Feedback Conversation After Continuity Test

If passed:
> "I was able to manage your work without contacting you. This is the standard. Keep it up."

If failed:
> "I couldn't answer [specific questions] from your CRM records. This means if you were sick tomorrow, we would lose [specific information]. Let's go through what needs to change."

Then go through each gap together and rebuild the habit.

# EVALUATION 4 — THE ROLE REVERSAL

**Purpose:** Confirm genuine understanding by requiring the employee to teach Grekam OS to an evaluator acting as a new joiner.

**When:** End of Month 1.

**Duration:** 20 minutes teaching, 10 minutes Q&A.

**Setup:** The evaluator acts as a brand new employee who has never used Grekam OS.

---

## Instructions to the Employee

> "Imagine I am starting at the company today. You have 20 minutes to teach me how to use Grekam OS for my role. I will ask questions as if I were a genuine new joiner. Go."

No slides. No notes. Live product.

---

## Questions to Ask During the Role Reversal

Throw these in naturally during their teaching:

**Easy (should answer immediately):**
> "Why can't I just track my leads in an Excel sheet?"
> "What do I do when a customer calls with a complaint?"
> "How do I know which leads I need to follow up today?"

**Medium (should think, then answer):**
> "I just sent a proposal to a client. What stage should the deal be in?"
> "The client said 'I'll think about it.' What do I do in Grekam OS?"
> "I finished a task but the client asked for one more change. What do I do?"

**Hard (tests genuine understanding):**
> "My manager is on leave and the client needs an update on their project. How do I find everything I need?"
> "I made a mistake and quoted the wrong price to a client. What is the correct way to handle this?"
> "I've been here 2 months and nobody checks my CRM. Why should I still update it?"

---

## Role Reversal Scoring

| Criteria | Strong | Acceptable | Needs Work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explains the purpose of CRM, not just the steps | ✅ | | |
| Can navigate the system confidently without fumbling | ✅ | | |
| Answers unexpected questions with reasoning | ✅ | | |
| Teaches the "why" not just the "how" | ✅ | | |
| Handles the hard questions with a thoughtful answer | ✅ | | |

**PASS:** Strong or Acceptable in 4 out of 5 criteria.

**FAIL:** "Needs Work" in 3 or more criteria. Additional 2 weeks of practice and re-test.

# EVALUATION 5 — WEEKLY CRM HEALTH CHECK

**Purpose:** Ongoing quality control. Ensures no employee's standards drop after initial training.

**When:** Every Friday, 15 minutes.

**Conducted by:** Operations Manager / BDM.

**Method:** Open each salesperson's pipeline in Grekam OS and run through the health check.

---

## Health Check Template

```
WEEKLY CRM HEALTH CHECK
Date: _______________
Reviewed by: _______________
Employee: _______________

LEADS
[ ] All leads in valid pipeline stage
[ ] No lead with no next action
[ ] No follow-up date in the past without a note
[ ] All leads spoken to this week have updated notes
[ ] Closed Lost leads all have a reason recorded

DEALS
[ ] All active deals have an expected close date
[ ] No deal stuck in same stage for 7+ days without a note
[ ] Pipeline value is realistic

TASKS
[ ] No task overdue by more than 1 day without a note
[ ] All completed tasks are marked complete
[ ] No vague task descriptions ("call client" without context)

PROJECTS (if applicable)
[ ] All projects updated in last 48 hours
[ ] Status is accurate (On Track / At Risk / Delayed)
[ ] No unreported deadline risks

OVERALL RATING
[ ] Clean — no issues
[ ] Minor gaps — flagged, no immediate action needed
[ ] Significant gaps — conversation required
[ ] Critical — immediate correction needed

Notes for employee:
_________________________________________________
_________________________________________________
```

---

## Health Check Conversation Guide

**If Clean:**
> "Your pipeline looks current and complete. Good discipline."

**If Minor Gaps:**
> "I noticed [specific gap]. Can you update that today?"

**If Significant Gaps:**
> "Let's sit down for 10 minutes. Your CRM has [specific issues] and I want to understand what happened."

**If Critical:**
> Formal conversation. Document the gaps. Set a 48-hour correction deadline. Re-check on Monday.

# CERTIFICATION SCORECARD

Each employee earns Grekam OS certification at the end of Month 1 based on all five evaluations.

---

## Final Certification Rubric

| Evaluation | Weight | Pass | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 7 Setup Test | 20% | 5/6 correct | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| Week 3 Live Audit | 20% | 4/5 questions from CRM | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| Month 1 Continuity Test | 30% | 4/5 questions from CRM | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| Role Reversal | 30% | 4/5 criteria strong/acceptable | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |

---

## Certification Outcome

**CERTIFIED — Level 1 (User):** Passed all 4 evaluations.

> The employee can use Grekam OS correctly and independently for their role. They are trusted to maintain CRM discipline without supervision.

---

**CONDITIONAL PASS:** Passed 3 of 4. One evaluation failed.

> The employee may proceed to active work but must re-test the failed evaluation within 2 weeks. Manager reviews their CRM weekly until full certification is achieved.

---

**NOT CERTIFIED:** Failed 2 or more evaluations.

> Additional 2-week training period. Re-test all failed evaluations. Manager countersigns all entries daily for 2 weeks until standard is demonstrated.
