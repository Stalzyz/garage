# SECTION 1 — PER-PRODUCT FINANCIAL METRICS

We define our pricing, direct costs, margins, and target acquisition limits for each of our 4 products.

---

## 1. Grafty (WhatsApp Automation)

Grafty is a SaaS-model recurring revenue product.

* **Pricing Packages:**
  * Starter: ₹1,999/month
  * Business: ₹4,500/month
  * Enterprise: Custom (Avg: ₹12,000/month)
* **Blended ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** ₹3,800/month
* **Direct Monthly Cost (Meta API support + server overhead):** ₹500/month (excludes Meta API usage charges which are paid direct by client)
* **Gross Margin:** 86.8% (₹3,300/month)
* **Target CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** ₹5,000 (Payback period: 1.5 months)
* **LTV (Lifetime Value Estimate):** ₹45,600 (Assumes 12-month average retention)
* **Breakeven Point (Independent):** **15 active customers** (covers dedicated infrastructure costs of ₹50,000/month)

---

## 2. Atlas (Ecommerce Platform)

Atlas is a hybrid product with an upfront setup fee and a recurring subscription fee.

* **Pricing Packages:**
  * Setup Fee (Standard): ₹25,000 (One-time)
  * Setup Fee (Growth): ₹45,000 (One-time)
  * Setup Fee (Enterprise): Custom (Avg: ₹1,20,000)
  * Monthly Subscription (Standard/Growth/Ent blended): ₹2,500/month
* **Blended Year 1 ARPU:** ₹55,000 (Setup fee + 12 months subscription)
* **Direct Setup Cost (Developer time + onboarding support):** ₹12,000 (One-time)
* **Direct Monthly Cost (Hosting + payment integrations):** ₹300/month
* **Gross Margin (Year 1):** 71.6% (₹39,400)
* **Target CAC:** ₹10,000 (Payback period: Immediate on setup fee)
* **Breakeven Point (Independent):** **5 setups/month** (covers direct developer salary allocation of ₹60,000/month)

---

## 3. Web Development (Custom Redesign / Redirection)

Web Development is a service/project model product with high cash-flow generation.

* **Pricing Packages (Standard builds):**
  * Brochure Website: ₹15,000 to ₹25,000
  * Lead-Gen Website: ₹35,000 to ₹60,000
  * Custom Web App: ₹1,50,000+
* **Blended Average Contract Value (ACV):** ₹45,000 (One-time)
* **Direct Cost (Developer + Designer project allocation):** ₹18,000
* **Gross Margin:** 60.0% (₹27,000 per project)
* **Target CAC:** ₹8,000 (Acquisition through outbound/referrals)
* **Breakeven Point:** **3 projects/month** (covers delivery team overhead of ₹80,000/month)

---

## 4. Grekam OS (Business Operations Platform)

Grekam OS is our core enterprise SaaS product, sold to larger teams.

* **Pricing Packages:**
  * Starter (up to 5 users): ₹3,500/month
  * Business (up to 15 users): ₹7,500/month
  * Enterprise: ₹15,000+/month
* **Blended ARPU:** ₹6,800/month
* **Direct Monthly Cost (Server infrastructure + priority support):** ₹800/month
* **Gross Margin:** 88.2% (₹6,000/month)
* **Target CAC:** ₹12,000 (Payback period: 2 months)
* **Breakeven Point:** **10 active customers** (covers system maintenance overhead of ₹60,000/month)

# SECTION 2 — PRODUCT MIX STRATEGY

To optimize cash flow and valuation, we run a dual-engine product mix:

```
CASH ENGINE
Web Development & Atlas Setup Fees
High upfront cash flow → Funds operations and developer salaries directly.
          ↓
SCALE ENGINE
Grafty, Atlas, & Grekam OS Subscriptions (MRR)
Compounding monthly recurring revenue → Builds company valuation and stability.
```

### Strategic Action Rules:
1. **Never sell Web Dev at a loss to get recurring business.** Each project must be individually profitable (minimum 50% margin).
2. **Prioritise Grafty for volume.** It has the lowest onboarding overhead and fastest sales cycle (typically 7 days).
3. **Use Atlas as the entry point for traditional retail.** Once they pay the setup fee, they are locked into the Grekam ecosystem, making the Grafty upsell natural.

# SECTION 4 — MANAGEMENT AUDIT PROTOCOL

If we miss our targets in any month:

1. **Check Lead Flow:** Did we generate the qualified leads required by the sales conversion model?
2. **Check CAC:** Are we spending too much to acquire customers, eroding our gross margin?
3. **Check Churn:** If active customer counts drop, identify the failure in the **Customer Onboarding SOP**.
4. **Adjust Product Mix:** If cash flow is low, BDM shifts focus to Web Dev / Atlas setups. If MRR is flat, BDM shifts focus to Grafty and Grekam OS subscriptions.
