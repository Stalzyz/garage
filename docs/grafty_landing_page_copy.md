## Frequently Asked Questions

* **Can I keep my current WhatsApp number?**
  > Yes. You can migrate your existing business number to the official Meta Business API. Note that once migrated, the number will be accessed through the Grafty dashboard, not the standard mobile WhatsApp app.
* **How long does approval take?**
  > Number registration takes 5 minutes. Meta template approvals take anywhere from 10 minutes to 2 hours. Business verification (optional but recommended) takes 1–3 business days.
* **Are there long-term contracts?**
  > No. All plans are month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your dashboard.
