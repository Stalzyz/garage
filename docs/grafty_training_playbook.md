# SECTION 1 — Product Overview

## What is Grafty?
Grafty is a business communication platform built on top of the **official Meta WhatsApp Business API**. It allows companies to scale, automate, and organize customer conversations.

### Key Distinction: WhatsApp App vs. WhatsApp API (Grafty)

| WhatsApp Business App | Grafty (WhatsApp API) |
|---|---|
| Limited to 1 phone + 4 linked web devices | Unlimited team members on any device |
| Manual messaging only | Advanced automated chat flows & auto-replies |
| Max 256 contacts per broadcast (often marked spam) | Unlimited broadcast campaigns to opted-in lists |
| No detailed analytics | Deep insights on delivery, read rates, & agent performance |
| High ban risk for sending business updates | Zero ban risk for approved templates |

# SECTION 2 — Core Value Propositions

## 1. Automation (Saves Time)
- Auto-replies handle routine enquiries (location, timing, catalogue, price list) 24/7.
- Integration allows automatic updates for order placement, payment links, and shipping status.

## 2. Multi-User Team Inbox (Collaboration)
- Multiple agents can log into the same official business number from different locations.
- Managers can assign conversations to specific team members and monitor performance.

## 3. Bulk Broadcasting (Engagement)
- Send updates, festival offers, and announcements to your entire customer database securely.
- Track read, reply, and click rates to see what converts.

# SECTION 4 — The 10-Minute Demo Script

Follow this structure to run a high-converting Grafty demonstration.

### Phase 1: Setup & Warmup (2 Mins)
- Open the Grafty demo dashboard.
- Introduce the interface:
  > *"Think of this as a unified control center for all your business communication. This is your shared team inbox."*

### Phase 2: The Core Workflow — Shared Inbox (3 Mins)
- Show a message arriving live in the inbox.
- Assign it to an agent:
  > *"Look at this message from a customer. I can assign it to Priya with one click. Priya handles the query, but as the manager, you can see the entire conversation history in real time. Your customer data never leaves the company."*

### Phase 3: The Magic Moment — Automation & Templates (3 Mins)
- Trigger a quick reply template.
- Show how variable fields populate (e.g., name, order ID):
  > *"Instead of typing out standard responses, your team clicks one button. It pulls up a pre-approved template, customises the name automatically, and sends it instantly. This keeps your communication professional and saves hours daily."*
- Demonstrate a broadcast screen:
  > *"You can filter contacts (e.g., 'Skincare Buyers') and schedule a campaign to 2,000 customers in 30 seconds."*

### Phase 4: Closing / Ask (2 Mins)
- Close the demo environment. Ask:
  > *"How would a system like this change your team's daily workload?"*
- Pivot to pricing packages.

# SECTION 6 — Grafty Objection Handling Cheat-Sheet

## "Why do I have to pay Meta API fees separately?"
> *"Meta charges per conversation (24-hour windows). We pass these direct billing charges to you with zero markup. This ensures you only pay for what you actually use rather than us inflating your monthly subscription."*

## "Is my personal number safe? Will it get banned?"
> *"Since we integrate with the official Meta API, your number is fully whitelisted. We use pre-approved templates for outgoing messages, which means you have 0% risk of account suspension."*
