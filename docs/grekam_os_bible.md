## 2.1 — LEADS

### What is a Lead?
A lead is any individual or business that has expressed interest in our products or services, or that we are actively pursuing, but has not yet become a paying customer.

### When to Create a Lead
Create a lead the moment you:
- Receive an enquiry through any channel (WhatsApp, Instagram, phone, email, referral, walk-in)
- Begin outreach to a potential prospect
- Receive a referral from an existing customer

**Do not wait.** Create the lead while the conversation is fresh. Do not batch-enter leads at the end of the day.

### Required Fields for Every Lead

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | First and last name. No "Ramesh sir" or "Client". |
| **Company Name** | Business name. If individual, write their profession. |
| **Phone** | Primary contact number with country code if international |
| **Source** | Where did this lead come from? (Instagram / WhatsApp / Referral / Cold Call / Website / Walk-in / Event) |
| **Requirement** | What did they ask about? Be specific. (Not "website" — write "ecommerce website for clothing brand, 500+ products, Instagram integration") |
| **Lead Status** | Current stage in the pipeline |
| **Next Action** | What happens next? Must be specific. |
| **Next Follow-up Date** | The exact date and time you will contact them next |
| **Notes** | Any context that matters — budget mentioned, decision-maker identified, objections raised, timeline expressed |

### Lead Status Options

```
NEW LEAD       — Just created, not yet contacted
CONTACTED      — First contact made, conversation started
QUALIFIED      — Budget, requirement, and decision-maker confirmed
DEMO SCHEDULED — Product demo appointment confirmed
DEMO COMPLETED — Demo done, follow-up in progress
PROPOSAL SENT  — Formal quotation sent to client
NEGOTIATION    — Client is reviewing, discussing price or scope
PAYMENT PENDING — Deal agreed, awaiting payment
CLOSED WON     — Payment received, moving to onboarding
CLOSED LOST    — Not proceeding. Must log reason.
ON HOLD        — Client asked to pause, specific resume date noted
```

### Closed Lost — Always Log the Reason

When a lead is marked Closed Lost, you must record why:

- Price too high
- Chose a competitor (name the competitor if known)
- Project cancelled
- No budget currently
- No response after multiple follow-ups
- Wrong fit — requirement we cannot fulfill
- Other (explain)

Lost deal data is how we improve.

### The Next Action Rule

Every active lead must have a next action.

Not: `Call later`
Yes: `Call Tuesday 19 Aug at 11am — confirm whether budget has been approved internally`

If there is no next action, the lead is being neglected.

## 2.2 — CONTACTS AND CUSTOMERS

### What is a Contact?
A contact is a person. A customer is a company or individual who has paid for our services.

When a lead converts (payment received), they move from Lead to Customer.

### Customer Record Requirements

Every customer record must contain:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Business name | Full legal or trading name |
| Industry | What sector they operate in |
| Products purchased | Which of our products/services they use |
| Onboarding date | When they became a customer |
| Account manager | Who internally is responsible for this account |
| Payment status | Up to date / Outstanding / Overdue |
| Renewal date | When their subscription or contract renews |
| Notes | Anything relevant to the relationship |
| Escalation history | Any complaints or issues previously raised |

### The Customer Is Never Just a File

Every customer record should tell a story. If someone new reads it, they should understand:
- What the customer bought
- How the onboarding went
- What their common concerns are
- What opportunities exist for upsell or renewal
- What problems have been resolved

## 2.3 — DEALS AND PIPELINE

### What is a Deal?
A deal represents a specific sales opportunity with a specific customer for a specific product, value, and expected closing date.

One customer may have multiple deals (e.g., they buy a website now and Atlas later).

### Required Fields for Every Deal

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| Deal Name | Descriptive: "Ramesh Traders — Grafty Starter" not "Deal 1" |
| Value | Expected revenue from this deal in rupees |
| Stage | Current pipeline stage |
| Expected Close Date | When you realistically expect payment |
| Product | Which product or service |
| Contact | Linked to the customer or lead contact |
| Probability | Your realistic estimate of winning (%) |
| Notes | What is happening in this deal right now |

### Pipeline Review Expectations

Every salesperson is expected to be able to answer, from Grekam OS:
- How many active deals do I have?
- What is my total pipeline value?
- What is expected to close this month?
- Which deals have had no activity in the last 7 days?
- Which deals are past their expected close date?

If you cannot answer these questions from the system, the data is not up to date.

## 2.4 — TASKS

### What is a Task?
A task is any piece of work that needs to be done, has an owner, and has a deadline.

Tasks are how the company coordinates work without depending on verbal instructions.

### Every Task Must Have

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Clear, specific action verb. "Call Ramesh Traders" not "Ramesh" |
| **Assigned To** | One person responsible. Not "team". |
| **Due Date** | Specific date and time |
| **Priority** | Low / Medium / High / Urgent |
| **Description** | Context needed to complete the task without asking anyone |
| **Related To** | Link to the lead, deal, customer, or project it belongs to |

### Task Status

```
TO DO      — Not yet started
IN PROGRESS — Work has begun
BLOCKED    — Cannot proceed, reason documented in notes
COMPLETED  — Done. Mark as complete the moment it is done.
CANCELLED  — No longer needed, reason noted
```

### Overdue Tasks

An overdue task is a visible failure.

If you will miss a deadline:
1. Update the task with a note explaining why
2. Set a new realistic date
3. Inform the relevant person directly if the delay affects them

Never leave a task as overdue without a note.

### The Delegation Rule

When you assign a task to someone:
- The description must contain enough information to complete the work without asking you
- If you would need to explain the task verbally, the description is incomplete
- You remain responsible for the outcome even though someone else is doing the work

## 2.5 — PROJECTS

### What is a Project?
A project is a structured piece of delivery work for a customer. It is larger and longer than a task. It has phases, milestones, a team, and a delivery deadline.

### Every Project Must Have

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Project Name | Client name + deliverable: "Ramesh Traders — Atlas Ecommerce" |
| Client | Linked customer record |
| Project Manager | The single person accountable for delivery |
| Start Date | Actual start date |
| Delivery Deadline | Committed date to the client |
| Current Status | On Track / At Risk / Delayed / Completed |
| Phase | Which phase of delivery are we in |
| Progress % | Honest estimate of overall completion |
| Budget | Agreed project value |
| Notes | Current blockers, dependencies, recent updates |

### Project Updates

Every active project must be updated in Grekam OS at minimum every 48 hours.

Update format:
```
[Date] — [Progress %] — [What was completed] — [What is next] — [Any blockers]

Example:
14 Aug — 40% — Homepage and About page completed.
Next: Product catalogue pages.
Blocker: Client has not sent product images yet.
Follow-up scheduled with client: 15 Aug, 10am.
```

### Delay Reporting

If a project will be delayed, the project manager must:
1. Update the project status to "At Risk" or "Delayed" immediately
2. Document the reason
3. Propose a revised delivery date
4. Notify the client through the account manager before the original deadline

**Never wait for the deadline to pass before reporting a delay.**

## 2.6 — HR AND ATTENDANCE

### Attendance

All employees mark their attendance in Grekam OS every working day.

- Mark attendance at the start of your working day
- If you are absent or late, the reason is recorded
- Do not expect colleagues or managers to track your attendance for you

### Roles and Permissions

Grekam OS controls what each employee can see and do based on their role.

If you cannot access something you need:
1. Check if it is within your role's scope
2. If yes, raise an access issue with the Operations Manager
3. Do not ask other employees to share access with you

## 2.7 — FOLLOW-UPS

### The Follow-Up Is Not Optional

Every time you speak with a lead or customer, you must create a follow-up.

A follow-up without a date is not a follow-up. It is an intention.

### Follow-Up Entry Requirements

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Type | Call / WhatsApp / Email / Meeting / Demo |
| Outcome | What happened in this interaction |
| Next Step | What happens next as a result |
| Follow-up Date | The exact date you will next contact them |

### Follow-Up Cadence by Stage

| Lead Stage | Maximum Time Before Next Follow-Up |
|---|---|
| Contacted | 2 business days |
| Qualified | 3 business days |
| Demo Scheduled | 1 day before demo (confirmation call) |
| Demo Completed | 1 business day |
| Proposal Sent | 2 business days |
| Negotiation | 2 business days |
| Payment Pending | 1 business day |

If more time than this passes with no activity, the lead is being neglected.

## 2.8 — REPORTS

### Who Uses Reports

Reports are for management and for team leads. Every individual contributor should also review their own numbers weekly.

### Key Reports Every Salesperson Reviews Weekly

- **My Pipeline** — All active deals, their stages, values, and expected close dates
- **My Follow-ups Due** — What needs to be contacted today and this week
- **Overdue Tasks** — What should have been done but wasn't
- **This Month's Activity** — Calls, messages, demos, proposals, closed deals
- **Conversion Rate** — How many leads become qualified, how many become deals, how many close

### Key Reports Management Reviews Weekly

- **Pipeline by Salesperson** — Who has what in their pipeline
- **Revenue Forecast** — Expected closures this month and next
- **Lead Source Performance** — Which sources produce the best leads
- **Conversion Funnel** — Where leads are dropping out of the pipeline
- **Overdue Projects** — Which deliveries are at risk
- **Collections Status** — Which customers have outstanding payments

# SECTION 4 — Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

---

### Mistake 1: Creating a lead with incomplete information

**What happens:** Another person cannot follow up. The data is useless.

**Prevention:** Do not save a lead record unless all required fields are complete. If you don't have all information yet, use the Notes field to say "Company name to be confirmed. Following up."

---

### Mistake 2: Updating the lead status without updating the next action

**What happens:** The lead status says "Demo Completed" but there is no next follow-up date. The lead dies.

**Prevention:** Every status change must come with a new next action and date.

---

### Mistake 3: Writing vague tasks

**What happens:** The assigned person does not know what to do. They either do the wrong thing or ask for clarification (wasting time).

**Prevention:** Read your own task description before saving it. Ask: "If I received this task with zero context, would I know exactly what to do?" If no, rewrite it.

---

### Mistake 4: Marking a project as "On Track" when it is not

**What happens:** Management is blindsided at the deadline. The client is angry. Trust is damaged.

**Prevention:** Update the project status to "At Risk" the moment you have doubt about the deadline. Early warning is management's most valuable input.

---

### Mistake 5: Logging follow-ups after the fact, batched at the end of the week

**What happens:** You forget important context. Dates are wrong. The data is unreliable.

**Prevention:** Log every interaction within 10 minutes of it happening. Keep Grekam OS open during your workday, not as a reporting tool at the end.

---

### Mistake 6: Using personal WhatsApp or notes to store customer commitments

**What happens:** You take a day off. A customer escalates. Nobody can find the information. The company looks unprofessional.

**Prevention:** Any promise, commitment, or important piece of information shared in conversation must be immediately added to the relevant record in Grekam OS.

# SECTION 6 — The Continuity Standard

At any point in time, any team member should be able to pick up another person's work from Grekam OS alone — without making a phone call.

Ask yourself every day before you close Grekam OS:

> "If I were unavailable tomorrow, could my manager or colleague continue my work from what is in the system?"

If the answer is no, your day in Grekam OS is not finished.
