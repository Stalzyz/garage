# Implementation Plan — BDM Hiring, Financial Targets, and Landing Page Copy Redesign

We will design and write three core components of Grekam's business and marketing operations:
1. **BDM Hiring & Interview Process:** A comprehensive hiring guide including job description, values interview guide, role-play scenario exercises, and an evaluation scorecard.
2. **Financial Targets & Model:** A detailed financial breakdown for each of the 4 Grekam products (Grafty, Atlas, Web Dev, Grekam OS) including pricing, breakeven targets, ARPU, CAC, margin, and a month-by-month projection for the first 12 months.
3. **Product Marketing Landing Pages Copy:** Deep copy specifications, structural flow, direct-response headlines, section-by-section breakdowns, and pricing CTA positioning for:
   - **`grafty.pro`** (WhatsApp Bulk Messages & Automation)
   - **`https://atlas.grekam.in/build-ecommerce`** (Atlas Custom Build)
