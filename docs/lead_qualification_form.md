# Grekam — Lead Qualification Form
### Version 1.0 | Sales Team Reference | August 2026

---

> Qualification is not interrogation.
> It is respectful discovery.
> The goal is to understand whether this lead
> is the right fit for us, and whether we are the right fit for them.
>
> A qualified lead you lose is a better outcome than an unqualified deal you win.

---

# WHY WE QUALIFY

Unqualified leads waste:
- Your time
- The development team's time
- Management's time
- Company credibility when we cannot deliver what was promised

Qualified leads:
- Close faster
- Generate fewer post-sale complaints
- Produce better profit margins
- Create referrals

---

# PART 1 — UNIVERSAL QUALIFICATION QUESTIONS
*Ask these for every lead, regardless of product.*

---

## Section A — The Business

| Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| What does your company do? | Understand context before pitching |
| What industry are you in? | Helps with product selection and pitch angle |
| How long have you been operating? | Maturity signals budget and seriousness |
| Roughly how many people are in your team? | Size helps estimate complexity and decision-making |
| Are you a single location or multiple? | Affects scope and pricing |

---

## Section B — The Problem

| Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| What problem are you trying to solve right now? | The entire conversation should be built around this |
| How long has this been a problem? | Urgency signal — longer = higher pain = easier close |
| What are you currently using to handle this? | Existing tool = qualified lead who already values the solution |
| What's not working with your current approach? | Identifies the gap your product fills |
| What happens if this problem isn't solved in the next 3 months? | Creates urgency and shows consequence awareness |

---

## Section C — The Decision

| Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Who makes the final decision on this? | Speak to the right person or be prepared for delays |
| Are there other people involved in this decision? | Partners, finance, IT — know the landscape |
| Have you evaluated any other solutions? | Competitive awareness and urgency signal |
| What would make you confident to proceed? | Surface the real criteria for a yes |
| When are you looking to get started? | Timeline reveals seriousness |

---

## Section D — The Budget

> Budget conversations are sensitive. Never ask "what's your budget?" bluntly on the first call.
> Approach it through context.

**Approach 1 — Anchor with range:**
> "Our solutions typically range from around ₹X to ₹Y depending on the scope. Does that align with what you were thinking?"

**Approach 2 — Indirection:**
> "Are you working within a specific budget for this, or are you more focused on finding the right solution and evaluating the cost from there?"

**Approach 3 — Previous tool cost:**
> "What are you currently spending on [current solution or manual work]?"

**What you are looking for:**
- Budget range confirmed ✅
- Budget vague but reasonable for product ✅
- Budget clearly too low for our minimum ❌ — qualify out or offer the right package

# PART 2 — PRODUCT-SPECIFIC QUALIFICATION

---

## 2A — GRAFTY (WhatsApp Automation)

Ask after the universal questions when Grafty is the relevant product.

| Question | Signal |
|---|---|
| How many WhatsApp messages does your business send or receive per day? | Volume = justification for automation |
| How many staff currently manage your WhatsApp? | Staff cost = ROI argument |
| Do you use WhatsApp Business or personal WhatsApp? | Business = more qualified; personal = needs migration conversation |
| Are you sending any broadcasts or bulk messages currently? | Yes = already understands use case |
| Do you have a verified WhatsApp Business account? | No = onboarding complexity increases |
| What types of messages do you send most? (Enquiries / Orders / Updates / Support) | Maps to Grafty features to demonstrate |
| Have you heard of the WhatsApp Business API? | Awareness level = calibrates your explanation depth |
| What's your main goal — automation, organisation, or customer communication? | Prioritises which Grafty features to lead with |

**Minimum to qualify for Grafty:**
- At least 20+ WhatsApp messages per day
- At least 1 person managing WhatsApp full-time or part-time
- A business purpose that fits (retail, services, ecommerce, F&B, education)

**Disqualify if:**
- They want to use personal WhatsApp only and refuse to migrate
- Volume is fewer than 10 messages per day (ROI too low to justify)
- They need features that WhatsApp Business API does not support

## 2B — ATLAS (Ecommerce Platform)

| Question | Signal |
|---|---|
| Are you currently selling online? | Yes = already values ecommerce |
| What platform are you using now? (Shopify / Instagram / WhatsApp / None) | Identifies migration vs. fresh start |
| How many products do you have? | Scale = determines plan/complexity |
| Do you need to manage inventory? | Yes = Atlas core feature |
| What payment methods do you need? (UPI / Cards / COD / International) | Integration scope |
| Do you need to manage multiple warehouses or locations? | Complexity signal |
| Do you need customer accounts / loyalty / reviews? | Feature scope |
| Who will manage the store on a daily basis? | Training need |
| Do you need the storefront to match your existing brand? | Design customisation requirement |
| Are you planning any integrations? (Accounting / WhatsApp / CRM) | Development scope |

**Minimum to qualify for Atlas:**
- Active intention to sell online
- Product catalogue ready or in progress
- Decision-maker identified and engaged

**Disqualify if:**
- They want a marketplace (like Amazon or Flipkart) not their own store
- They have under 5 products and no plans to scale
- Their requirement is fully custom and outside Atlas capabilities without major development

---

## 2C — WEB DEVELOPMENT

| Question | Signal |
|---|---|
| Do you have an existing website? | No = new build; Yes = redesign or enhancement |
| What is the primary purpose of the new website? | Brochure / Lead gen / Ecommerce / Booking / Application |
| Do you need users to log in or create accounts? | Yes = web application complexity |
| Do you need payment processing? | Yes = additional integration |
| Do you have existing brand guidelines, logo, colours? | Yes = faster design phase |
| Do you have content ready? (Text, photos, copy) | No = adds scope and timeline |
| What pages do you need? | Scope definition |
| Do you need the website to integrate with any other system? | CRM / WhatsApp / Payment / Inventory |
| Do you have a hosting or domain already? | No = we handle or advise |
| What is your timeline to launch? | Urgency and feasibility check |
| Who approves designs on your end? | Stakeholder clarity for delivery |

**Minimum to qualify for Web Development:**
- Clear understanding of what they want (not just "a good website")
- Realistic timeline (minimum 3 weeks for basic, 6–12 weeks for ecommerce/application)
- Budget aligned with scope
- Decision-maker present in the sales conversation

**Disqualify or flag for review if:**
- They say "just copy this website" — scope may be deceptively large
- Timeline is unrealistic without a conversation about trade-offs
- They want a price without being willing to scope the project

## 2D — GREKAM OS (Internal Operations Platform)

| Question | Signal |
|---|---|
| How many people are in your team right now? | Seat count affects pricing |
| What are you currently using to manage leads and customers? | Excel = high pain = easy to convert |
| Do you have a defined sales pipeline? | No = we help build one |
| How do you currently track tasks and projects? | WhatsApp = high opportunity |
| Do you have HR or attendance management in place? | No = broader Grekam OS value |
| Who would be the primary admin of the platform? | Training target identified |
| Are you open to changing your current process? | Resistance = flag early |

**Minimum to qualify for Grekam OS:**
- At least 3 to 5 active team members
- Current tools are insufficient (Excel, WhatsApp, notebooks)
- A lead, task, or customer management pain is clearly articulated

# PART 3 — DISQUALIFICATION CHECKLIST

Mark a lead as **Closed Lost — Not a Fit** if any of the following are true:

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Budget is clearly below minimum and no scope adjustment possible | Politely close |
| Requirement cannot be fulfilled by any of our products | Refer or close |
| Decision-maker refuses to engage — only junior staff involved | Put on hold, escalate contact |
| Timeline is unrealistic and client is unwilling to adjust | Discuss, then close if no movement |
| Client wants ownership of source code as a base expectation | Escalate to management |
| Client has a history of non-payment with other vendors (if known) | Flag to management |
| Requirement is legally questionable | Escalate immediately |

# PART 4 — LEAD SCORING

Use this to prioritize your time across multiple active leads.

Score each lead from 1 to 5 on each dimension:

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Clarity** | Vague, undefined | General idea | Specific, urgent problem |
| **Budget** | No budget discussion | Budget uncertain but possible | Budget confirmed and appropriate |
| **Authority** | No decision-maker access | Decision-maker aware | Decision-maker engaged directly |
| **Timeline** | No timeline mentioned | Within 6 months | Within this month |
| **Product Fit** | Weak match | Decent match | Strong, obvious match |

**Total Score:**
- **20–25** → Hot lead. Prioritise daily.
- **13–19** → Warm lead. Active follow-up every 2–3 days.
- **8–12** → Developing lead. Weekly follow-up. Build the relationship.
- **Below 8** → Qualify further or put on hold. Do not invest heavily until score improves.
