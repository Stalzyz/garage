You are joining Grekam in a Support role.

Support is not the last line of defence.

How you handle a frustrated customer in 10 minutes determines whether they stay for 3 years or cancel next month.

# 1. THE MOST IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE

## You solve problems. You do not forward problems.

The first reaction when a customer raises an issue must never be:
> "Let me check with the team."

The first reaction is:
> "Let me find out what happened and fix it."

Forwarding is the last resort. Investigation is the first step.

When a customer contacts support, they want:
1. To feel heard
2. To understand what happened
3. To have it resolved quickly
4. To trust it won't happen again

Your job is to deliver all four.

# 3. DAYS 1–15 — SHADOW EVERY SUPPORT INTERACTION

For your first two weeks, you sit with the existing account manager or senior support person for every customer interaction.

**What to study:**
- How they open a support conversation
- How they investigate before responding
- How they communicate a delay or bad news
- How they handle a customer who is angry
- How they decide to escalate vs. resolve independently
- How they document the resolution in Grekam OS

Do not respond to customers during this phase.
Do take detailed notes.
Do ask "why did you do that?" after every non-obvious decision.

# 4. HOW TO HANDLE EVERY SUPPORT TICKET

### Step 1 — Acknowledge (Within 4 hours, always)

Even if you do not have the answer:

> "Hi [Name], I've received your message and I'm looking into this now. I'll come back to you with an update by [specific time]."

Setting a time and meeting it builds more trust than a fast response followed by silence.

### Step 2 — Investigate (Before Responding)

Check in Grekam OS:
- Customer account, plan, and payment status
- Previous support interactions
- Onboarding notes
- Any recent changes or updates to the product

Check with the relevant team:
- Technical issue? → Tech team
- Billing or account issue? → Operations
- Delivery or project issue? → PM

### Step 3 — Respond With a Resolution, Not a Question

**Weak response:**
> "Can you tell me more about the problem?"

**Strong response:**
> "I've checked your account. The issue is that your message template was rejected by Meta because the category was set to 'Marketing' when it should be 'Utility.' I've updated it and resubmitted. You'll see approval within 24 hours. Let me know if you need anything else."

The difference: one response requires the customer to do more work. The other solves the problem.

When you genuinely need more information:
> "To investigate this properly, I need one specific thing from you: [clear, single question]. Once you send that, I'll resolve it today."

One question. Specific. Not an open-ended "please describe the issue."

# 5. THE ESCALATION DECISION TREE

You own the decision of whether to escalate.

Before escalating, answer these questions:

```
Is this a product usage question?
    YES → Resolve yourself using product knowledge or documentation
    NO ↓

Is this a technical bug?
    YES → Log in Grekam OS, assign to tech with priority, 
           update customer, monitor resolution timeline
    NO ↓

Is this a billing or payment issue?
    YES → Check payment records, consult Operations if unclear,
           resolve with management approval for any adjustments
    NO ↓

Is this a scope dispute about a project?
    YES → Review the proposal. Check the handoff form.
           Escalate to BDM with full context and your assessment.
    NO ↓

Is the customer threatening legal action or a refund above policy limits?
    YES → Escalate immediately to management. Say nothing more to customer
           until management has reviewed.
    NO ↓

Handle it yourself.
```

**When you escalate, never say:**
> "I don't know. Let me ask someone."

**Always say:**
> "I want to make sure this is handled correctly. I'm involving [role] and will come back to you by [time]."

# 6. HANDLING ANGRY CUSTOMERS

When a customer is frustrated or angry:

### What to do:

**Step 1 — Let them finish.**
Do not interrupt. Do not defend. Do not explain while they are speaking.

**Step 2 — Acknowledge without assuming fault.**
> "I hear you and I understand why this is frustrating. Let me look into this properly."

Not: "I'm so sorry, we really messed up." (You haven't investigated yet.)
Not: "That's not possible." (Dismissive and often wrong.)

**Step 3 — Investigate and return with facts.**
Come back with a clear picture — what happened, why, and what will be done.

**Step 4 — Own the resolution.**
> "Here is what I'm doing to fix this and when it will be resolved."

Customers do not need the company to be perfect. They need the company to take responsibility and act.

**Step 5 — Follow up after resolution.**
48 hours later: "Just checking in to make sure everything is working well."

This single step converts more frustrated customers into loyal ones than any discount or apology.

# 7. CUSTOMER TRAINING

You will sometimes need to train customers on how to use our products.

### Before a Training Session

- Know what specific thing they are trying to do (one objective per session)
- Know their current level of understanding (beginner / intermediate)
- Have the product open and ready before the call starts
- Prepare a short step-by-step walkthrough

### During a Training Session

- Show, do not tell. Screen share and demonstrate.
- Ask them to repeat the action while you watch
- Do not skip steps because they seem obvious
- End every session with: "What questions do you have?" (Not "Is everything clear?" — that gets a yes even when it isn't)

### After a Training Session

- Send a follow-up message summarising what was covered
- If you explained the same thing twice — record it once as a video or write it as a FAQ
- Log the session in the customer record in Grekam OS

**The rule: anything you explain more than twice should become a document.**

# 8. MONTH 2 — INDEPENDENT RESOLUTION

By the end of Month 2, you should be resolving 80% of tickets independently without asking anyone for help.

**Month 2 expectations:**
- Acknowledge every ticket within 4 hours
- Resolve Level 1 and L2 issues independently
- Make correct escalation decisions on L3 and above
- Every ticket documented in Grekam OS with outcome
- No customer waits more than 24 hours for a substantive update

**Month 2 self-check:**
- How many tickets did I resolve without escalating?
- How many escalations were genuinely necessary vs. avoidable?
- How many customers followed up because I hadn't updated them?

# 9. MONTH 3 — BUILD THE SELF-SERVICE SYSTEM

The best support is the support that doesn't need to happen.

Your Month 3 assignment: reduce the volume of repetitive tickets by building resources that answer them before customers ask.

### What to Build

**FAQ Document (per product):**
- The 10 most common questions for Grafty
- The 10 most common questions for Atlas
- The 10 most common questions for Grekam OS

**How-to Guide Library:**
- One short written guide for each common task in each product
- "How to create a Grafty template" — step by step with screenshots
- "How to process a return in Atlas" — step by step

**Known Issues Register:**
- A living document of known bugs or limitations
- Updated whenever a new recurring issue is identified
- Shared with the tech team so they know what to prioritise

**When these exist**, new support agents can onboard in half the time.
When these exist, some customers resolve their own issues.

# 10. YOUR DAILY OPERATING ROUTINE

### Morning
- Check all open tickets in Grekam OS
- Prioritise by severity and waiting time
- Update any customer who has been waiting since yesterday before doing anything else

### During the Day
- Process tickets in order of severity
- Log every interaction, every outcome
- Raise technical issues to the dev team with full reproduction steps

### End of Day
- Every open ticket has an update or a scheduled follow-up
- No customer has been waiting more than 24 hours without communication
- Escalations that need management attention are flagged before you leave

# 12. TEN RULES FOR SUPPORT AT GREKAM

1. **Every ticket is owned by one person until it is resolved.**
2. **Acknowledge within 4 hours. Always. No exceptions.**
3. **Investigate before you respond. Never respond to fill silence.**
4. **Solve the problem, do not forward the problem.**
5. **Document every interaction in Grekam OS.**
6. **If you explained something twice, write it down.**
7. **Follow up 48 hours after every major resolution.**
8. **Angry customers are not difficult customers — they are urgent customers.**
9. **Escalate with a recommendation, not just a problem.**
10. **Your job is not to close tickets. Your job is to retain customers.**
