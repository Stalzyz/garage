# Grekam — WhatsApp Script Library
### Version 1.0 | Sales Team Reference | August 2026

---

> WhatsApp is not informal.
> Every message represents the company.
> Write professionally. Write briefly.
> Always end with a clear next step or question.

---

# RULES BEFORE USING THESE SCRIPTS

1. **Personalise every message.** Replace all [BRACKETS] before sending.
2. **Do not copy-paste identically to every lead.** Adjust the language to match the conversation you've had.
3. **Keep it short.** If your message takes more than 15 seconds to read, it is too long.
4. **One message, one purpose.** Do not combine a greeting, a pitch, a follow-up, and a question into one message.
5. **Log every WhatsApp interaction in Grekam OS** immediately after sending.

---

# MESSAGE 1 — FIRST CONTACT (Cold Outreach)

*Sent to a lead you have not spoken to before. Use only when a call was not answered or when reaching out via WhatsApp first.*

---

**Version A — After a missed call:**

> Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Grekam. I tried calling a moment ago but couldn't connect. I'll try again [tomorrow / later today], but wanted to introduce myself in the meantime.
>
> We work with businesses like yours on WhatsApp automation, ecommerce platforms, and digital tools. Happy to share more if it sounds relevant.
>
> Have a good day.

---

**Version B — Direct outreach (referral or enquiry):**

> Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Grekam. [Referral name] / I came across your business and thought what we do might be useful for you.
>
> We help businesses with [one specific relevant benefit — e.g., "automating WhatsApp customer communication" or "building ecommerce stores"].
>
> Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call this week?

---

# MESSAGE 2 — FOLLOW-UP AFTER A CALL

*Sent within 30 minutes of a call where you spoke to the lead.*

---

> Hi [Name], great speaking with you just now.
>
> As discussed, I'll be [sending you details / setting up a demo / preparing a proposal] and will follow up by [specific day and time].
>
> If you have any questions in the meantime, feel free to reach out here.
>
> [Your Name] | Grekam

# MESSAGE 3 — DEMO CONFIRMATION

*Sent after a demo is booked. Send within 10 minutes of booking.*

# MESSAGE 5 — PROPOSAL DELIVERY

*Sent when a formal proposal is being shared.*

---

> Hi [Name], I've put together the proposal we discussed.
>
> [Attach PDF]
>
> Quick summary:
> - Product: [Name]
> - Plan: [Plan name]
> - Investment: ₹[Amount] [per month / one-time]

# MESSAGE 6 — FOLLOW-UP AFTER PROPOSAL (No Response)

*Sent 2 business days after the proposal if no reply.*

---

**First follow-up:**

> Hi [Name], I wanted to follow up on the proposal I sent on [Day].
>
> Happy to go through any part of it together if that would help — it usually takes about 10 minutes.
>
> When would be a good time to connect?

---

**Second follow-up (if still no response after 2 more days):**

> Hi [Name], I know things get busy. Just checking in one more time on the proposal.
>
> If the timing isn't right, that's completely fine — just let me know and I'll follow up at a better time.
>
> Otherwise, happy to answer any questions whenever you're ready.

---

**Third follow-up (final, after 5+ days of silence):**

> Hi [Name], I don't want to keep messaging if this isn't the right time.
>
> I'll leave it here for now. If you'd like to revisit this in the future, feel free to reach out — I'm happy to help.
>
> [Your Name] | Grekam

---

# MESSAGE 7 — PAYMENT FOLLOW-UP

*Sent when a deal is agreed but payment has not come through.*

---

**First reminder (1 day after agreement):**

> Hi [Name], just wanted to confirm the next steps. Once the payment is processed, our team can begin onboarding [by Day / within X days].
>
> If you need the payment link again or have any questions about the process, just let me know.

---

**Second reminder (3 days after):**

> Hi [Name], following up on the payment for [Product]. Is everything okay on your end?
>
> If there's anything holding it up, happy to help sort it out.

---

**Third reminder (5+ days — escalation tone, still professional):**

> Hi [Name], I want to make sure we haven't lost momentum. We agreed to move forward with [Product] on [Date].
>
> Could you confirm when the payment will be processed so I can schedule the onboarding?
>
> If something has changed, let me know — happy to discuss.

# MESSAGE 9 — RE-ENGAGEMENT (Cold Lead — 30+ Days of Silence)

*Sent to a lead that went cold without a clear reason.*

---

**Version A — Soft re-engagement:**

> Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] from Grekam. We spoke a while back about [Product / topic].
>
> I know the timing wasn't right at that point. Just checking in to see how things are going on your end.
>
> Has anything changed that might make this relevant again?

---

**Version B — Value-led re-engagement:**

> Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] from Grekam.
>
> I was thinking about our earlier conversation and came across something that might be useful for [their specific situation / industry].
>
> [One sentence on new feature / relevant case study / industry insight]
>
> Worth a quick chat? Happy to keep it short.

---

**Version C — Direct and honest:**

> Hi [Name], I don't want to keep messaging without a reason.
>
> If you're still open to exploring [Product], I'd love to schedule 15 minutes. If the timing isn't right, just let me know and I'll close the conversation on my end.
>
> Either way, no hard feelings.

# QUICK REFERENCE — TONE GUIDELINES

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| First contact | Warm, brief, curious |
| Demo booking | Efficient, confirming, professional |
| Proposal delivery | Confident, structured, clear |
| Payment follow-up | Friendly, then progressively direct |
| Re-engagement | Respectful, no pressure, curious |
| Post-sale | Warm, welcoming, reassuring |
| Handling a complaint | Calm, empathetic, solution-focused |

---

# WHAT NOT TO SEND ON WHATSAPP

❌ Long paragraphs of product features  
❌ Generic "Hope you are doing well, just checking in" with no purpose  
❌ Forwarded brochures without a personal note  
❌ Multiple messages in a row (send one, wait for a reply)  
❌ Messages at odd hours (before 9am or after 8pm)  
❌ Angry or passive-aggressive messages when a customer goes quiet  
❌ Price negotiations over WhatsApp without a prior call
